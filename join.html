Join as a Member

Lost Limb Riders is a community for amputees, people with limb difference, families, caregivers, veterans, riders, non-riders, supporters, and allies who believe in dignity, connection, resilience, and service.

Begin the membership process

What membership means

Membership begins with a request to participate and an initial program enrollment. Members help build a community where people show up, treat one another with respect, follow program and safety expectations, and contribute to the culture they want to receive.

Membership is its own relationship. It does not create employment, a volunteer assignment, governance or approval authority, permission to represent the organization, or system access.

How the process begins

1

Express interest

Contact Lost Limb Riders and tell us you want to become a member. The public first step asks only for the basic information needed to respond.

2

Review and enrollment

A Membership Coordinator follows up about the confidential program-enrollment process, applicable consent, and the program that fits your request.

3

Membership decision

The organization reviews eligibility and required acknowledgments. An approved application is activated as a Member relationship and initial program enrollment.

Ready to take the first step?

Send a membership-interest message. Do not send medical records, identity documents, Social Security numbers, banking details, or other restricted information by email.
