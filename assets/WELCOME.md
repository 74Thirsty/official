# Welcome to Lost Limb Riders

**Dear Friend,**

Welcome to **Lost Limb Riders**, and thank you for becoming part of our community.

We are genuinely glad you're here.

Lost Limb Riders was founded on a belief that strength is not measured by what life takes away from us, but by what we choose to do with what remains. Our mission is to build community, encourage independence, raise self-esteem, reduce isolation, and remind people living with limb loss and disabilities that their story is far from over.

## Your Welcome Gift

As a new subscriber, you will receive a **free autographed copy of *I Can, I Will***, the autobiography of **John Thompson**.

This personally signed autobiography shares John's experiences, challenges, setbacks, determination, and journey through circumstances that could have stopped him—but didn't.

We are proud to include this book as a special welcome gift for our subscribers. We hope that as you read *I Can, I Will*, you find encouragement in its pages and a reminder that sometimes the most important step forward begins with believing that you can take it.

**I Can, I Will**

Those words represent more than the title of an autobiography. They represent the determination to keep moving forward when life tells you that you cannot.

## Welcome to the Community

As a Lost Limb Riders subscriber, you'll receive our newsletter and updates about what we're doing, including:

* Community news and announcements
* Upcoming rides and events
* Stories and experiences from our community
* Opportunities to participate and get involved
* Organizational programs and initiatives
* Important Lost Limb Riders updates

We want you to feel connected—not simply subscribed.

Whether you're directly affected by limb loss or disability, know someone who is, ride with us, support our mission, or simply believe in what we're trying to accomplish, **you are welcome here.**

Thank you for joining Lost Limb Riders and for giving us the opportunity to share this journey with you.

We look forward to having you along for the ride.

**Ride strong. Stand together. Keep moving forward.**
