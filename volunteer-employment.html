Volunteer or Work With Us

Volunteering and employment are different relationships with separate review, approval, records, and onboarding. Choose the path that matches your interest.

Volunteer pathway

Volunteer with Lost Limb Riders

Approved volunteer service may support events, outreach, member care, hospitality, photography, hospital packet assembly, fundraising, sponsor relations, accessibility support, communications, transportation where specifically authorized, or administration.

Interest is followed by an application and review. Screening depends on the actual assignment and its risks. Approval, orientation, a defined assignment, a named supervisor, and any required training come before service begins.

Employment pathway

Employment with Lost Limb Riders

No approved openings are posted on this site

Lost Limb Riders does not treat employment interest as volunteer onboarding. A paid position must be authorized before recruitment and follows its own selection, compensation approval, offer, employment onboarding, and record process.

You may express general interest for future consideration. This is not an application for a current opening or a promise of employment.

Express employment interest

Protect your information

The public first step is only an expression of interest. Do not email Social Security numbers, banking or payroll information, tax forms, identity documents, background-check records, medical records, or other restricted information. Any later controlled process will explain how required information is handled.
