Compliance Engine

Predefined compliance workflows are organized by organizational activity. Each workflow is built from a controlled template with a predefined set of applicable sections, and carries a versioned, hash-anchored definition (derived from ADM-REF-002 — the Transaction Map). Start a workflow, complete its stages, and the engine records evidence, approvals, controlled documents and signatures, registers, and an audit trail.
