Hospital & Prosthetic Partnership Program

A community-support pathway that helps healthcare and prosthetic professionals connect amputees, people with limb difference, and families with Lost Limb Riders.

Discuss a referral or collaboration

For amputees and patients

A hospital, rehabilitation team, prosthetic provider, wound-care provider, discharge planner, case manager, social worker, or another professional can introduce Lost Limb Riders as an available community resource.

With your consent, that connection can open the door to peer support, community, resource navigation, and follow-up outside clinical care. You may also contact Lost Limb Riders directly.

Learn about peer support

For providers

Lost Limb Riders welcomes conversations with organizations that want an informed community resource for appropriate individuals and families.

  • Recognize when community or lived-experience support may help.
  • Explain the option and obtain the individual’s consent before sharing information.
  • Coordinate referrals and approved visits through the designated Lost Limb Riders contact.
  • Protect privacy and follow facility requirements.
Start a provider conversation

Who can connect with us

Hospitals, trauma and rehabilitation facilities, prosthetic and orthotic providers, wound-care providers, physical and occupational rehabilitation teams, discharge planners, case managers, social workers, patient advocates, and other appropriate healthcare organizations.

No partnership is implied by this page. Formal relationships, facility access, patient information exchange, and visits require appropriate approval, consent, privacy safeguards, and facility-specific protocols.

A community resource—not clinical care

Lost Limb Riders provides peer and community support. It does not replace medical care, rehabilitation, prosthetic care, licensed mental-health treatment, or emergency services. Clinical decisions remain between the individual and qualified providers.
